## Bottom line

1. The best hook has arrived, and it is bigger than the prior pack said. Anak Krakatau's ash closed Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta (CGK) and seven other airports from 6 to 8 September 2026. The government counted 2,961 flights and about 341,000 people affected across all the closed airports, not CGK alone [CONFIRMED-X2: https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/06/travel/anak-krakatau-indonesia-eruption-airports-intl-hnk]. The airport called it the first ash closure in CGK's history [verified 2026-10-10: Kompas frames it as the first closure caused by volcanic ash "sepanjang sejarah"; "first full closure ever" rests only on the spokesman's informal "Iya, baru pertama kali" and is not independently confirmed. The closure also ran as a chain of NOTAMs (A3346/26, A3348/26, A3368/26 revised by A3373/26, A3387/26), not one block: https://megapolitan.kompas.com/read/2026/09/08/16193401/penutupan-bandara-soekarno-hatta-imbas-abu-vulkanik-jadi-pertama-kali].
2. During that closure the official sources did disagree, and on the record. The volcano observatory's only aviation notice in those three days (VONA 2026KRA026, RED, 5 Sep 02:00Z) says "Ash-cloud is not observed." It had no successor until 2026KRA027 at 8 Sep 04:34Z [CONFIRMED: https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona?code=KRA, accessed 2026-10-10]. In the same period the met agency BMKG issued 39 SIGMETs and reported ash to 50,000 ft [CONFIRMED: https://www.bmkg.go.id/siaran-pers/imbas-sebaran-abu-vulknaik-anak-krakatau-8-bandara-ditutup-sementara-bmkg-minta-waspada-dan-tenang, accessed 2026-10-10].
3. The disagreement is still live this week. At 18:00Z on 9 October, the Semeru VONA says "Ash-cloud is not observed." At the same minute, Darwin VAAC advisory 2026/1157 and the Ujung Pandang FIR SIGMET 12 say ash is observed to FL150, moving east [CONFIRMED: https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona, https://www.bom.gov.au/products/Volc_ash_recent.shtml, https://aviationweather.gov/api/data/isigmet?format=json&hazard=va, all accessed 2026-10-10].
4. The SIGMET is not an independent source. Both Indonesian SIGMETs open tonight copy the Darwin VAA polygon vertex for vertex. The real disagreement is observatory vs ash centre (plus satellite and wind), not three-way.
5. Nothing found in July to October 2026 undercuts the idea fatally. No airline, Google or VAAC announced an ash-reconciliation or per-flight ash advisory tool. Three items need handling in the pitch:
   - ICAO's quantitative volcanic ash (QVA) becomes due from all VAACs on 26 November 2026 [CONFIRMED: https://assets.ctfassets.net/krj50g99u3hm/56ZjQ3Omw7OpcUMpfpFll8/6057af9b554a9de8c5698dc06e55c34b/Joint_QVA_Task_Force_-_QVA_Implementation_Timeline.pdf, accessed 2026-10-10].
   - EUROCONTROL's EVITA already computes which flights ash affects, in Europe (same source).
   - Dispatch vendors shipped AI features in 2026 (The Weather Company for Breeze; Jeppesen), none about ash.
6. One prior-pack fact is wrong in a way that would hurt the build. The JMA page `vaac_darwin_list.html` is Tokyo's "Darwin VAAC Backup" list, not a mirror of Darwin advisories. It only fills when Tokyo stands in for Darwin, as it did on 23 Sep 2026 [CONFIRMED: https://ds.data.jma.go.jp/svd/vaac/data/vaac_darwin_list.html, accessed 2026-10-10]. The working keyless source for all Darwin (and all other VAAC) advisories is the Bureau of Meteorology's 7-day page (HTTP 200 today).

## 1. Anak Krakatau and the Jakarta closure, 4-8 September 2026

### 1.1 Timeline

- The Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM) says a period of continuous eruption began at 23:07 local time on Friday 4 Sep and ended about 25 hours later, at 00:04 on Sunday 6 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/flights-canceled-jakarta-airport-anak-krakatau-eruption-disrupts-regional-travel-volcano.html, accessed 2026-10-10].
- The US Embassy dates the eruption to "the evening of September 5, 2026". It says CGK and Halim were closed until 09:30 and Lampung until 10:00 on 6 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://id.usembassy.gov/natural-disaster-alert-mt-anak-krakatau-eruption-september-6-2026/, accessed 2026-10-10].
- The Jakarta Post (agencies) says it "erupted on Saturday (September 5)" [CONFIRMED: https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/09/07/soekarno-hatta-airport-extends-closure-after-anak-krakatau-eruption, accessed 2026-10-10]. AP says it "erupted early Sunday", with eruptions of 30 seconds at 3:53 a.m. and 16 seconds about three hours later per the Geological Agency [CONFIRMED: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/volcanic-island-in-indonesia-erupts-forcing-the-cancellation-of-hundreds-of-flights, accessed 2026-10-10]. Four dates for one eruption, depending on which pulse each source counts.
- Indoneo reports that CGK's paper ash tests turned positive between 00:35 and 01:05 WIB on 6 Sep. It reports that NOTAM A3341/26 closed the aerodrome from 01:30 WIB, and that AirNav said the airspace "remained closed from 04:52 WIB" [REPORTED: https://www.indoneo.com/earth/jakarta-airport-anak-krakatau-ash-closure-september-2026/]. The ash on the airfield came before AP's 3:53 a.m. pulse; ESDM's continuous-eruption window explains it.
- BMKG analysed two ash clusters at 04:00 local on 6 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://en.tempo.co/read/2119084/6-indonesian-airports-remain-closed-as-anak-krakatau-ash-disrupts-flights, accessed 2026-10-10].
- At least six successive "closed until" times were published:
  - 09:30 on 6 Sep (US Embassy).
  - 21:30, then midnight, on 6 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://asianews.network/jakarta-airport-closure-extended-as-anak-krakatau-volcano-eruption-disrupts-flights/, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - 08:59 on 7 Sep [CONFIRMED: Tempo URL above; https://www.cbsnews.com/news/indonesia-volcano-eruption-flights-airport/, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - 18:00 on 7 Sep [CONFIRMED: Jakarta Post URL above; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/6/indonesias-main-airport-suspends-flights-due-to-anak-krakatoa-eruption, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - 23:59 on 7 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-07/australians-stranded-as-volcanic-ash-closes-jakarta-airport/107125502, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - Search-result headlines also show "until 13.30" (Kompas, 6 Sep) and "17.30" (Serambinews) [UNVERIFIED: https://megapolitan.kompas.com/read/2026/09/06/11360771/update-bandara-soekarno-hatta-ditutup-hingga-pukul-1330-akibat-abu].
- CGK reopened in the early hours of Tuesday 8 Sep, after paper tests came back negative [CONFIRMED: Kompas 8 Sep URL above]. Four other airports reopened the same day, one had reopened on Monday, and two stayed closed [CONFIRMED: CNN URL above].

### 1.2 Impact numbers (all as published; they measure different scopes)

- CGK is "Southeast Asia's second-busiest airport by scheduled airline seat capacity this month, with 3.24 million seats" (OAG) [CONFIRMED: CNBC URL above].
- Changi Airport Group: all 33 Singapore-Jakarta flights on 6 Sep cancelled. SIA cancelled 19 and Scoot 12. CGK had 301 flights disrupted, including 58 international (Angkasa Pura). Two Jakarta-bound flights from Jeddah and Madinah diverted to Kertajati. Garuda cancelled CGK flights until noon on 7 Sep [CONFIRMED: Asia News Network / Straits Times URL above].
- AP, Sunday evening: 1,558 cancellations and 170,000 passengers across eight airports, 963 of them at CGK. Bali and Medan were also disrupted [CONFIRMED: PBS/AP URL above].
- Transport Ministry: more than 270,000 passengers stranded, cumulative since 5 Sep. CGK: more than 240 flights cancelled or delayed on Monday morning, nearly 30,000 passengers [CONFIRMED: Jakarta Post URL above].
- Chief Infrastructure Minister Agus Harimurti Yudhoyono: "Around 341,000 people have been affected by the delay, diversion, or cancellation of 2,961 flights" [CONFIRMED: CNN URL above]. ABC: "Nearly 3,000 flights", "about 341,000 passengers" [CONFIRMED: ABC URL above].
- Transport Minister Dudy Purwagandhi: authorities "still needed to check on the airworthiness of the 178 planes that were at Soekarno-Hatta during the closure" [CONFIRMED: CNN URL above].
- Qantas cancelled QF39, QF40, QF41 and QF42 (Melbourne/Sydney-Jakarta) and offered fee-free changes for 5-11 Sep travel [CONFIRMED: ABC URL above].
- Migflug lists Malaysia Airlines, AirAsia, Lion Air Group and Garuda among cancelling carriers. It also cites InJourney: 209 CGK cancellations between 01:30 and 09:30 on 6 Sep (160 domestic, 39 international, 10 cargo) [REPORTED: https://migflug.com/afterburner/anak-krakatau-eruption-indonesian-airports-closed-2026/].

### 1.3 Cost numbers

The breakdown is lost revenue Rp8-15 bn, extra fuel/crew/parking Rp1.5-3 bn, and passenger handling Rp0.5-1.2 bn. For a week he gave "Rp70 miliar sampai Rp130 miliar" [CONFIRMED: https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/news/20260908163436-4-766264/erupsi-anak-krakatau-bisa-bikin-maskapai-tekor-rp19-miliar-sehari, accessed 2026-10-10]. ### 1.4 What each official source said, and where they disagreed

- **Observatory (PVMBG/CVGHM, MAGMA VONA).** The public VONA archive shows exactly two Anak Krakatau notices between 24 Aug and 5 Oct.
  - VONA 2026KRA026, issued 20260905/0200Z, colour RED (previous orange). "Volcanic Cloud Height: Ash-cloud is not observed." Remarks: "Eruption and ash emission is continuing. Eruption recorded on seismogram with maximum amplitude 70 mm." Next notice: "A new VONA will be issued if conditions change significantly".
  - VONA 2026KRA027, issued 20260908/0434Z, colour ORANGE, again "Ash-cloud is not observed" [CONFIRMED: https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona/22575?signature=4bce6aa9ba49588f081932c1c7f70bf9e2ec6e6159818c722d6bd692cfc19289 and https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona/22593?signature=db900e48d358430906f21bca19dc4d6743f4aa81d32146855f4e5d77dfcdb72c, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - The two notice numbers are consecutive, so no numbered VONA came between them: 74.5 hours of RED with no cloud height.
  - Caveat: the public list shows only 20 Krakatau VONAs in 2026 up to 5 Sep, yet that notice is number 026. Six numbered notices from earlier in 2026 are not on the public list [CONFIRMED: https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona?code=KRA, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - The red notice came about 9 h 53 min after the 23:07 WIB start of the continuous eruption (16:07Z on 4 Sep, from the CNBC/ESDM time).
- **Met agency (BMKG, Soekarno-Hatta Meteorological Watch Office).**
  - Press release of 7 Sep: "39 dokumen Significant Meteorological Information (SIGMET) sejak erupsi pertama terjadi pada 4 September 2026" [CONFIRMED: BMKG URL above].
  - Layers in that release: SFC-15,000 ft spreading south-west to north-west, with the latest SIGMET (06:30-09:30 WIB) moving west at 15 kt; SFC-50,000 ft out over the Indian Ocean (same source).
  - RRI quoted BMKG as having issued 18 SIGMETs by 6 Sep [UNVERIFIED: https://rri.co.id/en/national/2711398/bmkg-intensifies-monitoring-of-anak-krakatau-eruption]. Koran Sulindo headlined 50 [UNVERIFIED: https://koransulindo.com/abu-vulkanik-anak-krakatau-menyebar-bmkg-terbitkan-50-sigmet].
- **Ash centre (Darwin VAAC).**
  - AP and CBS attribute the two plumes, 20,000 ft drifting north-east over Jakarta and 50,000 ft over Banten/Lampung/Bengkulu and the Indian Ocean, to "Indonesia's volcanology agency and the Darwin Volcanic Ash Advisory Centre" [CONFIRMED: PBS/AP URL above]. The volcanology agency's own VONA carried no height.
  - Migflug summarises Darwin advisories moving "from ash at 7,000 ft to a high-level eruption near 45,000 ft, then ash near 48,000 ft moving west at 93 km/h", with Himawari-9 cloud tops of -68°C [REPORTED: Migflug URL above]. I could not open the Darwin advisories for 5-8 Sep: the BoM page keeps 7 days only.
- **Direction of the lower plume.** AP: "drifted northeast" over Jakarta. CNBC (BMKG): "moving east". BMKG 7 Sep: south-west to north-west, moving west. Straits Times: the wind first blew west and then, per BMKG, "east at 5 knots at flight level 15,000 feet", which is why the closure was extended.
- **Top of the lower layer.** 20,000 ft (AP, Al Jazeera, CNBC) vs SFC-15,000 ft (BMKG 7 Sep) vs "18,000-20,000 feet" (Indoneo explainer).
- **Airport count.** Eight (BMKG, Jakarta Post, Straits Times). CBS lists CGK, Halim "plus four other airports" yet says eight. ABC says "seven others" and later "the remaining six airports".
- **Ground truth.** Reopening at CGK followed negative paper tests [CONFIRMED: Kompas 8 Sep URL above]. Migflug reports CGK's routine weather observation carried "VA" at 05:30 UTC on 6 Sep [REPORTED: Migflug URL above].
- **Alert level.** Level III ("Alert") per CNBC and Reuters/CNN in September. MAGMA's home page today still lists Anak Krakatau among the four Level III volcanoes [CONFIRMED: https://magma.esdm.go.id/, accessed 2026-10-10]. Xinhua (via The Star, 9 Oct) says it "remains at Level II", with a 2 km zone [CONFIRMED that the article says it: https://thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/10/09/indonesia039s-anak-krakatau-volcano-erupts-once-again-this-time-spewing-thick-ash-plume, accessed 2026-10-10]. MAGMA shows Xinhua's level is wrong.
- **No public post-event review.** I found no DGCA, AirNav, BMKG or InJourney review of the closure after 8 Sep. Searches of Indonesian outlets returned operational coverage only (see Search log).

## What this supports

- **A real, fresh and very large APAC event.** First-ever full ash closure of CGK, 2,961 flights, about 341,000 people, 178 aircraft needing airworthiness checks, and Rp40 bn airport-operator loss for two days. INACA estimates Rp10-19 bn per day in airline losses.

- **Official sources disagreeing on the record, during the event.** VONA RED with "Ash-cloud is not observed" for 74.5 hours. BMKG issued 39 SIGMETs with ash to 50,000 ft. Lower-plume tops were 15,000 vs 20,000 ft and directions NE vs E vs W across agency statements within 24 hours. SIGMET counts of 18, 39 and 50 appeared in different outlets.

- **The ash centre itself can disappear.** Tokyo took over Darwin's area north of 20S on 23 Sep 2026 with no "TEST" label.
- **Volume that a person cannot read by hand.** At least 3,385 Darwin advisories in 2026 for four Indonesian volcanoes, about 4 per day each for Semeru and Ibu, and MAGMA's 778 Lewotobi eruptions by 27 Aug [REPORTED].
- **Regional breadth across JAPAC in one quarter.** Java (CGK and seven others), Flores (Maumere, three closures in a week), Kamchatka ash reaching Alaska (10 Alaska Airlines cancellations), Vanuatu ash reaching Fiji (Fiji Airways cancellations), Taal on 8 Oct.
- **Ground truth exists and decided the outcome.** CGK reopened after negative paper tests, and METAR carried "VA" [REPORTED]. This gives TEPHRA a fourth, non-satellite evidence type that judges can understand.
- **Google is not in this niche.** WeatherNext 3 and the Gemini agent (9 Oct) give a natural Google-stack story without a competing Google ash product.
