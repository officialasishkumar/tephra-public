## Bottom line

1. The best hook has arrived, and it is bigger than the prior pack said. Anak Krakatau's ash closed Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta (CGK) and seven other airports from 6 to 8 September 2026. The Chief Infrastructure Minister counted 2,961 flights and about 341,000 people affected across all eight closed airports, not CGK alone [CONFIRMED-X2: https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/06/travel/anak-krakatau-indonesia-eruption-airports-intl-hnk] [verified 2026-10-10: this is the highest of several official tallies. InJourney gave 1,547 flights and 194,451 passengers, InJourney's Agus Haryadi said "lebih dari 200 ribu" on 2 Oct, and the Transport Ministry gave about 270,000 cumulative. Cite 341,000 only with its source and its eight-airport scope]. It was the first full closure of CGK in at least 35 years, and the first ever for ash [verified 2026-10-10: Kompas frames it as the first ash closure "sepanjang sejarah", and spokesman Yudistiawan said "Iya, baru pertama kali" and "Covid itu tidak tutup loh". InJourney operations director Agus Haryadi separately said "menutup Bandara Soekarno-Hatta secara keseluruhan pertama kali", but his statement covers only his time there since 1992, and CGK opened in 1985. The closure also ran as a chain of NOTAMs (A3346/26, A3348/26, A3368/26 revised by A3373/26, A3387/26), not one block: https://megapolitan.kompas.com/read/2026/09/08/16193401/penutupan-bandara-soekarno-hatta-imbas-abu-vulkanik-jadi-pertama-kali; https://cnbcindonesia.com/news/20261002132746-4-772882/bandara-ditutup-efek-erupsi-anak-krakatau-angkasa-pura-rugi-rp-40-m].
2. During that closure the observatory gave no ash height and no update. Its only aviation notice in those days (VONA 2026KRA026, RED, 5 Sep 02:00Z) says "Ash-cloud is not observed" under cloud height, while its own remarks say "Eruption and ash emission is continuing." The next notice, 2026KRA027 (ORANGE), came at 8 Sep 04:34Z, 74.6 hours later. That gap contains the whole CGK closure, and RED stayed in force about 11.5 hours after CGK had reopened on negative paper tests [CONFIRMED-X2: https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona?code=KRA]. In the same period BMKG issued 39 SIGMETs (18 of them by 6 Sep), with ash from the surface to 15,000 ft around Java and a separate surface-to-50,000 ft layer far out over the Indian Ocean [CONFIRMED-X2: https://www.bmkg.go.id/siaran-pers/imbas-sebaran-abu-vulknaik-anak-krakatau-8-bandara-ditutup-sementara-bmkg-minta-waspada-dan-tenang] [verified 2026-10-10: the earlier wording "reported ash to 50,000 ft" overstated it. The 50,000 ft layer lay "lebih jauh di Samudra Hindia ... serta menjauhi wilayah Indonesia", and the latest SIGMET in the release (06:30-09:30 WIB, 7 Sep) gave ash "hingga 15.000 kaki". The observatory did not deny ash. Frame this as "no height and no update for 74.6 hours", not as agencies contradicting each other].
3. The same gap is live this week, but it is a gap in what each source can observe, not a dispute. At 18:00Z on 9 October (01:00 local, at night), the Semeru VONA says "Ash-cloud is not observed." At the same minute, Darwin VAAC advisory 2026/1157 and the Ujung Pandang FIR SIGMET 12 say ash is observed to FL150, moving east [CONFIRMED-X2: https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona, https://www.bom.gov.au/products/Volc_ash_recent.shtml, https://aviationweather.gov/api/data/isigmet?format=json&hazard=va] [verified 2026-10-10: the Darwin VAA lists "INFO SOURCE: HIMAWARI-9, CVGHM", so the ash centre already takes the observatory as an input, and it adds "AREA IS PARTIALLY OBSCURED BY MET CLOUD". A ground observer cannot see a cloud at 01:00. Pitch it as a cloud height that the dispatcher has to get from satellite, not as two agencies disagreeing].
4. The SIGMET is not an independent source. All four VA SIGMETs open over Indonesia tonight (WAAF 11 Dukono, WAAF 12 Semeru, WAAF 13 Ibu, WIIF 07 Krakatau) copy the Darwin VAA observed polygon vertex for vertex, and they drop the VAA's +6/+12/+18 h forecast polygons [CONFIRMED-X2: https://aviationweather.gov/api/data/isigmet?format=json&hazard=va, https://www.bom.gov.au/products/Volc_ash_recent.shtml]. The evidence that is independent of the VAA is the observatory's ground view, satellite pixels, winds, METAR "VA" and paper tests.
5. Nothing found is fatal, but prior art is closer than the first pass found. The claim "nobody does per-flight ash for dispatch" is wrong and must not appear in the pitch:
   - FlySafe.zone, a commercial service aimed at airline OCC and dispatch, fuses observatory colour codes, satellite thermal anomalies and VAAC advisories over 1,196 volcanoes, intersects ash polygons with FIRs, and sells an API that turns them into a 0-100 exposure index per route [VERIFIER: https://www.flysafe.zone/tools/volcanic-ash-map/]. See C11.
   - The Weather Company, the vendor behind Breeze's Maverick Dispatch, sells volcanic ash Flight Plan Guidance, "enhanced volcanic ash SIGMET" alerts and an "Enroute Hazards package". Its ash page was updated on 9 Sep 2026, right after Krakatau [VERIFIER: https://www.weathercompany.com/blog/volcanic-ash-and-aviation-safety-protecting-planes-passengers/]. See C6.
   - A 2026 Devpost project, "MeteoWatch - The AeroGuard AI", intersects aircraft trajectories with SIGMET areas and has an LLM write per-flight warnings [VERIFIER: Devpost, URL not recorded]. See C12.
   - ICAO recommends that all VAACs issue quantitative volcanic ash (QVA) forecasts from 26 November 2026 [CONFIRMED-X2: https://assets.ctfassets.net/krj50g99u3hm/56ZjQ3Omw7OpcUMpfpFll8/6057af9b554a9de8c5698dc06e55c34b/Joint_QVA_Task_Force_-_QVA_Implementation_Timeline.pdf] [verified 2026-10-10: the earlier wording "becomes due from all VAACs" overstated it. It is an Annex 3 Recommended Practice ("should"), not a Standard. European operational use is best case Q2 2027, and Asia-Pacific readiness, including Darwin's, is unconfirmed].
   - EUROCONTROL's EVITA already computes which flights ash affects, in Europe only, and its QVA-fed version is read-only (same source, CONFIRMED-X2).
   What we did not find is a product that reconciles VONA, VAA, SIGMET, satellite and ground tests, explains where and why they differ, and drafts a per-flight advisory that a dispatcher approves. That is the ground TEPHRA has to stand on.
6. One prior-pack fact is wrong in a way that would hurt the build. The JMA page `vaac_darwin_list.html` is Tokyo's "Darwin VAAC Backup" list, not a mirror of Darwin advisories. It only fills when Tokyo stands in for Darwin, as it did on 23 Sep 2026 [CONFIRMED-X2: https://ds.data.jma.go.jp/svd/vaac/data/vaac_darwin_list.html]. The working keyless source for all Darwin (and all other VAAC) advisories is the Bureau of Meteorology's 7-day page (HTTP 200 today). [verified 2026-10-10: the backup products keep Darwin's identity ("FVAU01 ADRM", "VAAC: DARWIN"), so headers do not show that Tokyo issued them. Merge the two feeds by (volcano, advisory number).]

## 1. Anak Krakatau and the Jakarta closure, 4-8 September 2026

### 1.1 Timeline

- The Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM) says a period of continuous eruption began at 23:07 local time on Friday 4 Sep and ended about 25 hours later, at 00:04 on Sunday 6 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/flights-canceled-jakarta-airport-anak-krakatau-eruption-disrupts-regional-travel-volcano.html, accessed 2026-10-10].
- The US Embassy dates the eruption to "the evening of September 5, 2026". It says CGK and Halim were closed until 09:30 and Lampung until 10:00 on 6 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://id.usembassy.gov/natural-disaster-alert-mt-anak-krakatau-eruption-september-6-2026/, accessed 2026-10-10].
- The Jakarta Post (agencies) says it "erupted on Saturday (September 5)" [CONFIRMED: https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/09/07/soekarno-hatta-airport-extends-closure-after-anak-krakatau-eruption, accessed 2026-10-10]. AP says it "erupted early Sunday", with eruptions of 30 seconds at 3:53 a.m. and 16 seconds about three hours later per the Geological Agency [CONFIRMED: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/volcanic-island-in-indonesia-erupts-forcing-the-cancellation-of-hundreds-of-flights, accessed 2026-10-10]. Four dates for one eruption, depending on which pulse each source counts.
- Indoneo reports that CGK's paper ash tests turned positive between 00:35 and 01:05 WIB on 6 Sep. It reports that NOTAM A3341/26 closed the aerodrome from 01:30 WIB, and that AirNav said the airspace "remained closed from 04:52 WIB" [REPORTED: https://www.indoneo.com/earth/jakarta-airport-anak-krakatau-ash-closure-september-2026/]. The ash on the airfield came before AP's 3:53 a.m. pulse; ESDM's continuous-eruption window explains it.
- BMKG analysed two ash clusters at 04:00 local on 6 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://en.tempo.co/read/2119084/6-indonesian-airports-remain-closed-as-anak-krakatau-ash-disrupts-flights, accessed 2026-10-10].
- At least six successive "closed until" times were published:
  - 09:30 on 6 Sep (US Embassy).
  - 21:30, then midnight, on 6 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://asianews.network/jakarta-airport-closure-extended-as-anak-krakatau-volcano-eruption-disrupts-flights/, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - 08:59 on 7 Sep [CONFIRMED: Tempo URL above; https://www.cbsnews.com/news/indonesia-volcano-eruption-flights-airport/, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - 18:00 on 7 Sep [CONFIRMED: Jakarta Post URL above; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/6/indonesias-main-airport-suspends-flights-due-to-anak-krakatoa-eruption, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - 23:59 on 7 Sep [CONFIRMED: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-07/australians-stranded-as-volcanic-ash-closes-jakarta-airport/107125502, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - Search-result headlines also show "until 13.30" (Kompas, 6 Sep) and "17.30" (Serambinews) [UNVERIFIED: https://megapolitan.kompas.com/read/2026/09/06/11360771/update-bandara-soekarno-hatta-ditutup-hingga-pukul-1330-akibat-abu].
- CGK reopened in the early hours of Tuesday 8 Sep (about 00:01 WIB, which is 17:01Z on 7 Sep), after paper tests came back negative [CONFIRMED-X2: Kompas 8 Sep URL above]. Four other airports reopened the same day, one had reopened on Monday, and two stayed closed [CONFIRMED-X2: CNN URL above].
- The observatory's aviation colour code lagged the airport. VONA stayed RED until 2026KRA027 at 04:34Z on 8 Sep (11:34 WIB), about 11.5 hours after CGK reopened [verified 2026-10-10: both verifiers; MAGMA KRA list and detail pages]. This is a concrete "signals out of sync" moment for the demo.

### 1.4 What each official source said, and where they disagreed

- **Observatory (PVMBG/CVGHM, MAGMA VONA).** The public VONA archive shows exactly two Anak Krakatau notices between 24 Aug and 5 Oct.
  - VONA 2026KRA026, issued 20260905/0200Z, colour RED (previous orange). "Volcanic Cloud Height: Ash-cloud is not observed." Remarks: "Eruption and ash emission is continuing. Eruption recorded on seismogram with maximum amplitude 70 mm." Next notice: "A new VONA will be issued if conditions change significantly".
  - VONA 2026KRA027, issued 20260908/0434Z, colour ORANGE, again "Ash-cloud is not observed" [CONFIRMED-X2: https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona/22575?signature=4bce6aa9ba49588f081932c1c7f70bf9e2ec6e6159818c722d6bd692cfc19289 and https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona/22593?signature=db900e48d358430906f21bca19dc4d6743f4aa81d32146855f4e5d77dfcdb72c].
  - The two notice numbers are consecutive, so no numbered VONA came between them: 74.6 hours (74 h 34 min) of RED with no cloud height and no update [verified 2026-10-10: previously given as 74.5 h]. The gap contains the whole CGK closure, from 01:30 WIB on 6 Sep (18:30Z on 5 Sep) to 00:01 WIB on 8 Sep (17:01Z on 7 Sep). RED outlasted the closure by about 11.5 hours.
  - The observatory did not deny ash. KRA026's own remarks say "Eruption and ash emission is continuing ... accompanied by booming sounds and air shockwaves". It gave no height. PVMBG eruption reports also gave the column height as "tidak teramati" (not observed) on 6, 8 and 9 Sep [VERIFIER: search summaries only]. The only height found for the 4-5 Sep pulses is a secondary IntelliNews figure of about 300 m above the summit, which is not in any VONA [VERIFIER: search summary].
  - Caveat: the public list shows only 20 Krakatau VONAs in 2026 up to 5 Sep, yet that notice is number 026. Six numbered notices from earlier in 2026 are not on the public list [CONFIRMED: https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/vona?code=KRA, accessed 2026-10-10].
  - The red notice came about 9 h 53 min after the 23:07 WIB start of the continuous eruption (16:07Z on 4 Sep, from the CNBC/ESDM time).
- **Met agency (BMKG, Soekarno-Hatta Meteorological Watch Office).**
  - Press release of 7 Sep: "39 dokumen Significant Meteorological Information (SIGMET) sejak erupsi pertama terjadi pada 4 September 2026" [CONFIRMED-X2: BMKG URL above]. It names the eight closed airports: Soekarno-Hatta, Halim, Radin Inten II, Husein Sastranegara, Budiarto, Pondok Cabe, Muhammad Taufiq Kiemas and Atungbusu, and says a "paper test pada pukul 07.00 WIB mengonfirmasi indikasi positif" (same source).
  - Layers in that release: SFC-15,000 ft spreading south-west to north-west, with the latest SIGMET (06:30-09:30 WIB) moving west at 15 kt; SFC-50,000 ft "lebih jauh di Samudra Hindia ... serta menjauhi wilayah Indonesia", that is, further out over the Indian Ocean and moving away from Indonesia (same source). ## What this supports

- **A real, fresh and very large APAC event.** First-ever full ash closure of CGK, 2,961 flights, about 341,000 people, 178 aircraft needing airworthiness checks, and Rp40 bn airport-operator loss for two days. INACA estimates Rp10-19 bn per day in airline losses.
- **Uncertainty that cost money by the hour.** At least six published "closed until" times in two days, plus an operator saying "We cannot ascertain when this will end due to the dynamic weather" (Transport Minister, ABC).
- **Official sources disagreeing on the record, during the event.** VONA RED with "Ash-cloud is not observed" for 74.5 hours. BMKG issued 39 SIGMETs with ash to 50,000 ft. Lower-plume tops were 15,000 vs 20,000 ft and directions NE vs E vs W across agency statements within 24 hours. SIGMET counts of 18, 39 and 50 appeared in different outlets.
- **The same disagreement is live on any given night.** Semeru 18:00Z on 9 Oct; Krakatau 9 Oct. A judge can open the feeds and see it.
- **The ash centre itself can disappear.** Tokyo took over Darwin's area north of 20S on 23 Sep 2026 with no "TEST" label.
- **Volume that a person cannot read by hand.** At least 3,385 Darwin advisories in 2026 for four Indonesian volcanoes, about 4 per day each for Semeru and Ibu, and MAGMA's 778 Lewotobi eruptions by 27 Aug [REPORTED].
- **Regional breadth across JAPAC in one quarter.** Java (CGK and seven others), Flores (Maumere, three closures in a week), Kamchatka ash reaching Alaska (10 Alaska Airlines cancellations), Vanuatu ash reaching Fiji (Fiji Airways cancellations), Taal on 8 Oct.
- **Ground truth exists and decided the outcome.** CGK reopened after negative paper tests, and METAR carried "VA" [REPORTED]. This gives TEPHRA a fourth, non-satellite evidence type that judges can understand.
- **Google is not in this niche.** WeatherNext 3 and the Gemini agent (9 Oct) give a natural Google-stack story without a competing Google ash product.
